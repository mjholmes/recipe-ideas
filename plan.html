Daily calories

Breakfast~300-330 kcal on weekdays, up to ~420 at the weekend
Snack~110-150 kcal
Lunch~280-405 kcal
Tea~480-580 kcal
Daily total~1,330-1,425 kcal (avg ~1,370)

Calorie counts are estimates from standard ingredient values and vary a little by brand and portion size. Quiche, venison sausages, halloumi, hollandaise and garlic bread vary the most, so check the packs. The bigger teas are paired with a lighter lunch or snack so each day stays balanced.

Weekday breakfasts are all quick or made the night before: granola two or three times a week, overnight oats, or peanut butter toast. The cooked breakfasts are at the weekend.

Batch cooking

  • Week 1, Tuesday: the ragu (T12) makes 4 portions. Save half for Wednesday's lunch and have it with a big salad instead of spaghetti.
  • Week 1, Thursday: cook about 250g extra chicken with T16 and slice it for Friday's wraps (L1).
  • Week 1, Friday: one batch of the spiced nut and seed mix (S12) makes about 8 portions, enough for both weeks.
  • Week 1, Saturday: the split pea soup (L8) makes 4 portions, for Saturday and Sunday lunch. It simmers for about an hour but needs little attention.
  • Week 2, Tuesday: save the extra chilli (T3) for Wednesday's lunch (L7).
  • Week 2, Wednesday: grill about 250g extra chicken with T8 for Thursday's wraps (L1).
  • Week 2, Saturday: the dhal (L9) makes 4 portions, for Saturday and Sunday lunch.

Tips for staying on track

  • Prep what you can the night before (overnight oats, chopped veg, couscous salads) to keep weekday mornings quick.
  • When you cook a batch, portion the extra into a container before serving, as it's easy to eat it by accident otherwise.
  • Measure the hollandaise and weigh the nut mix rather than pouring. Both are calorie-dense.
  • Drink plenty of water through the day. It's easy to mistake thirst for hunger.
  • If a portion leaves you hungry, add extra non-starchy veg or salad (peppers, cucumber, salad leaves, green beans) rather than extra carbs or fat.
  • Weigh in weekly rather than daily. Weight fluctuates day to day, and a weekly trend is a much more reliable guide.
