Calorie counts are estimates from standard ingredient values and vary a little by brand and portion size. Some of the newer teas (T9 to T16) run a little higher, at around 480-580 kcal, so pair those with a lighter lunch or snack where you can.

The chilli (T3) and bolognese (T7) are cooked as a bigger batch, so the extra portion becomes the next day's lunch (L7).

Tips for staying on track

  • Prep what you can the night before (overnight oats, boiled eggs, chopped veg) to keep weekday mornings quick.
  • When you cook the chilli or bolognese, portion the extra into a container before serving, as it's easy to eat it by accident otherwise.
  • Batch-cook the lentil soup in a bigger pot and freeze extra portions for more variety without more effort.
  • Drink plenty of water through the day. It's easy to mistake thirst for hunger.
  • If a portion leaves you hungry, add extra non-starchy veg or salad (peppers, cucumber, salad leaves, green beans) rather than extra carbs or fat.
  • Weigh in weekly rather than daily. Weight fluctuates day to day, and a weekly trend is a much more reliable guide.
