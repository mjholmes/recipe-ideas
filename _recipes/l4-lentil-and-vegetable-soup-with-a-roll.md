ingredients:
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 2 celery sticks, chopped
- 150g red lentils
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 wholemeal rolls
---

## Method

1. Sauté the onion, carrot and celery for 5 minutes.
2. Add the lentils, stock and tomatoes, simmer for 20 minutes until the lentils are soft.
3. Blend partially if you like a smoother soup. Serve with a roll.
4. Freezes well, good for batch-cooking ahead.
