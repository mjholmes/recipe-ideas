## Method

1. Toast the bread.
2. Mash the avocado with a squeeze of lemon juice and seasoning; spread over the toast.
3. Poach the eggs (3-4 minutes in gently simmering water) and place one on each slice.
4. Finish with a pinch of chilli flakes if you like.
