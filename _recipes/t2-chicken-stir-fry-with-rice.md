ingredients:

- 300g mixed stir-fry veg (peppers, broccoli, carrot)
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 160g rice (dry weight)
---

## Method

1. Cook the rice according to the packet.
2. Stir-fry the chicken until cooked through, then add the veg, garlic and ginger.
3. Stir-fry for 4-5 minutes, add the soy sauce, and serve over the rice.
