- 1 small swede (~400g), peeled and cubed
- 300g potatoes, peeled and cubed
- splash of skimmed milk
- black pepper, pinch of nutmeg
- green veg (cabbage, kale or peas)
- gravy granules, for a small jug of gravy
---

## Method

1. Boil the swede and potatoes in separate pans (or together, swede in first) for 15-20 minutes until soft; mash each with a splash of milk and plenty of pepper.
2. Meanwhile, grill or oven-bake the haggis slices per the pack (usually 10-15 minutes, turning once).
3. Steam the greens.
4. Serve the haggis with the neeps, tatties, greens and a little gravy.
