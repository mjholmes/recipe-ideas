- 2 venison leg steaks (~150g each, defrosted)
- 140g basmati rice (dry weight)
- 300g stir-fry veg pack (or sliced pepper, carrot, mangetout, spring onion)
- 2 garlic cloves
- thumb of ginger (or 2 tsp from a jar)
- 2 tsp oil
- 2 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce (split)
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp hoisin (or oyster sauce)

## Method

1. Slice the steaks thinly (about 5mm) across the grain. This is easier if they are still slightly frozen.
2. Toss with 1 tbsp soy and the cornflour, then put the rice on (about 12 minutes).
3. Mix the remaining soy, hoisin and 2 tbsp water in a cup.
4. Heat 1 tsp oil in a wok until smoking hot. Sear the venison in a single layer for 1-2 minutes, then lift out.
5. Add the remaining oil, fry the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then add the veg and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
6. Return the venison with the sauce and toss for 30 seconds until glossy. Serve straight away with the rice.

## Notes

- venison is very lean, so don't cook it any longer or it goes tough. Oyster sauce instead of hoisin saves about 25 kcal.
