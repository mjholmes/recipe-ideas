ingredients:
- 300g 5% fat beef mince
- 150g dried green lentils, rinsed (no soaking needed)
- 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stick, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp oil
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes, 1 tbsp tomato puree
- 300ml stock, 1 tsp dried oregano
- 60g dried spaghetti per portion
---

## Method

1. Soften the veg in the oil for 5 minutes, then add the mince and brown it.
2. Add the garlic, lentils, tomatoes, puree, stock and oregano. Simmer for 30-35 minutes until the lentils are tender, adding water if it gets dry.
3. Cook the spaghetti per the pack and serve with half the sauce.
4. Portion the other half for lunch or the freezer.

## Notes

- a slice of garlic bread each adds roughly 100 kcal (check the pack), taking it to about 635 kcal.
