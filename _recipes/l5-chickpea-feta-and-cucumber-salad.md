- 60g feta
- 1 cucumber, diced
- mixed salad leaves
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
---

## Method

1. Combine the chickpeas, crumbled feta, cucumber and salad leaves.
2. Dress with lemon juice and olive oil.
