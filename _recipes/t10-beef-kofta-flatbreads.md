- 1/2 onion, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp ground coriander, pinch of chilli
- 2 Greek flatbreads
- 100g fat-free Greek yogurt
- 5cm cucumber, grated and squeezed dry, plus a pinch of mint
- lettuce, sliced tomato and red onion
---

## Method

1. Mix the mince, onion, garlic, spices and seasoning. Shape into 6 sausage shapes.
2. Grill on high for 10-12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
3. Mix the yogurt, cucumber and mint to make a quick tzatziki.
4. Warm the flatbreads and fill with salad, koftas and tzatziki.
