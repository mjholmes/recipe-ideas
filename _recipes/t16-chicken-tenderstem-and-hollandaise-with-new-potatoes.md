- 500g new potatoes

- 1 tsp oil
- 4 level tbsp hollandaise sauce

## Method

1. Brush the chicken with the oil and bake or griddle for 20-25 minutes until cooked through.
2. Boil the potatoes for 15-18 minutes, adding the greens for the last 4.
3. Warm the hollandaise per the jar.
4. Slice the chicken and serve with the sauce spooned over.

## Notes

- hollandaise is calorie-dense, so measure it rather than pouring.
