ingredients:
- 2 venison leg steaks (~150g each, defrosted)
- 500g new potatoes, halved
- 200g green beans or tenderstem broccoli
- 2 tsp oil
- 2 tbsp redcurrant jelly
- 100ml beef stock
- splash of balsamic vinegar
- salt, pepper, thyme (optional)
---

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes for 15-18 minutes, adding the greens for the last 4 minutes.
2. Pat the steaks dry, season and rub with 1 tsp oil. Fry in a very hot pan for 2-3 minutes each side, venison is very lean, so keep it pink or it goes tough.
3. Rest for 5 minutes. Add the stock, jelly and balsamic to the pan and bubble for 2 minutes to make a glossy sauce.
4. Slice the steaks and serve with the potatoes, greens and sauce.
