- 2 peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- fajita seasoning
- 4 small wholemeal wraps
- low-fat soured cream (optional)
- salsa
---

## Method

1. Fry the chicken, peppers and onion with the fajita seasoning for 8-10 minutes until cooked through.
2. Serve in warmed wraps with salsa and a little soured cream.
