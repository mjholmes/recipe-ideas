## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 160°C fan / Gas 4.
2. Toss everything together on a tray and roast for 10-12 minutes, stirring halfway.
3. Cool and store in a jar.

## Notes

- weigh out 25g portions into small tubs, as it is very easy to overeat. The seeds also work sprinkled on porridge, salads or soup.
