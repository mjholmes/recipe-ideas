ingredients:
- 4 venison sausages
- 100g dried green lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp oil
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes, 1 tbsp tomato puree
- 400ml stock
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, pinch of thyme
- green veg, to serve
---

## Method

1. Brown the sausages in the oil for 5 minutes and lift out.
2. Soften the veg for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and paprika.
3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, puree, stock and thyme, return the sausages and simmer for 30-35 minutes until the lentils are soft.
4. Serve with steamed greens.

## Notes

- based on about 120 kcal per sausage. Venison sausages vary by brand, so check the pack.
