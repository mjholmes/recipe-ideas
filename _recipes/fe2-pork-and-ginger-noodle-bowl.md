- 2 nests medium egg noodles (~100g dry total)
- 300g stir-fry veg pack (or sliced pepper, carrot, cabbage, spring onion)
- 2 garlic cloves
- thumb of ginger (or 2 tsp from a jar)
- 2 tbsp reduced-salt soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sweet chilli sauce
- 1 tsp oil
---

## Method

1. Cook the noodles per the pack, then drain.
2. Fry the pork mince in 1 tsp oil over high heat, breaking it up, until browned (6-8 minutes); pour off any excess fat.
3. Add the garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute. Add the veg and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
4. Toss in the noodles, soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce, then serve.

## Notes

- if the mince is standard (15-20% fat) rather than 5%, use ~100g per person and bulk up the veg to keep portions in range.
