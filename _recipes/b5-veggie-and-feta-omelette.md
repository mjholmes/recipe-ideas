- 100g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 40g feta
- spray oil
- salt & pepper
---

## Method

1. Whisk the eggs and season.
2. Fry the mushrooms and tomatoes in a non-stick pan for 2-3 minutes.
3. Pour in the eggs, cook until nearly set, crumble the feta on top, fold and serve.
