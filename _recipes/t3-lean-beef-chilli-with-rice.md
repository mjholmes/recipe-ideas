ingredients:
- 500g lean beef mince (5% fat)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 pepper, chopped
- 1 tin kidney beans
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 240g rice (dry weight)
---

## Method

1. Brown the mince in a large pan.
2. Add the onion, garlic and pepper, cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add the beans, tomatoes and spices, simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Serve with rice, portion the extra straight into the fridge for tomorrow's lunch.
