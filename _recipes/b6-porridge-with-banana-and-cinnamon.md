ingredients:
- 80g porridge oats
- 400ml semi-skimmed milk (or water)
- 2 bananas
- pinch of cinnamon
- 2 tsp honey (optional)
---

## Method

1. Simmer the oats with the milk for 3-4 minutes, stirring, until creamy.
2. Top with sliced banana, cinnamon and honey if using.
