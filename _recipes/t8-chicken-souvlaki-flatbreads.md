ingredients:
- 2 white Greek-style flatbreads
- 300g chicken breast, cut into chunks
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp oil
- 100g fat-free Greek yogurt
- 5cm cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- pinch of mint, plus a little crushed garlic
- lettuce, sliced tomato and red onion
- 50g reduced-fat feta, crumbled
---

## Method

1. Toss the chicken with the lemon juice, garlic, oregano, oil and seasoning (leave 10 minutes if you have time).
2. Grill or griddle for 10-12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
3. Meanwhile, mix the yogurt, cucumber, mint and a little garlic to make a quick tzatziki.
4. Warm the flatbreads per the pack, then fill with salad, chicken, tzatziki and feta.

## Notes

- flatbreads vary by brand (roughly 216-262 kcal each), so check the pack. Warburtons soft flatbreads add about 30 kcal per portion.
