ingredients:
- 200g jumbo oats
- 2 ripe bananas, mashed (about 200g peeled)
- 30g unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 egg, beaten
- 50g raisins (or chopped dried apricots)
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds
- 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp vanilla extract, pinch of salt
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C / 160°C fan / Gas 4 and line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
2. Melt the butter and honey together in a small pan.
3. Mash the bananas in a large bowl, then stir in the egg, vanilla and the butter and honey mixture.
4. Add the oats, raisins, seeds, cinnamon and salt and mix well.
5. Press firmly into the tin and bake for 20-25 minutes until golden.
6. Score into 12 (a 4 x 3 grid) while hot and leave to cool completely in the tin.

## Notes

- one bar per person per snack. Keeps 5 days in the fridge; freeze half once cooled. Riper bananas give sweeter bars.
