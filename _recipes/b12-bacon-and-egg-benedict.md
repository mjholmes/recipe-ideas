## Method

1. Grill the bacon. Meanwhile, poach the eggs in gently simmering water for 3-4 minutes.
2. Toast the muffins and warm the hollandaise per the jar.
3. Top each muffin half with bacon and an egg, then spoon over the hollandaise.

## Notes

- a weekend brunch option. One egg each brings it down to about 345 kcal. Measure the hollandaise rather than pouring, as it is calorie-dense.
