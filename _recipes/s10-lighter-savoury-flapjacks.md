ingredients:
- 30g unsalted butter
- small bunch spring onions, sliced
- 200g jumbo oats
- 60g reduced-fat mature cheddar, grated
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds
- 2 carrots, grated
- 50g sun-dried tomatoes, chopped (if in oil, blot well on kitchen paper)
- 2 tsp wholegrain mustard
- 3 eggs, beaten
- salt and black pepper
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C / 170°C fan / Gas 5 and line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
2. Melt the butter in a small pan, add the spring onions and take off the heat.
3. Mix everything else in a large bowl, pour over the butter mixture and stir well. If it seems dry, add 2-3 tbsp milk.
4. Press firmly into the tin and bake for 22-25 minutes until golden and firm.
5. Score into 12 (a 4 x 3 grid) while hot and leave to cool completely in the tin.

## Notes

- one bar per person per snack. Keeps 5 days in the fridge in an airtight container; freeze half once cooled. Sun-dried tomato calories vary by brand, so check the pack.
