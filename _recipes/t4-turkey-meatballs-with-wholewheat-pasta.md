- 1 egg
- 30g breadcrumbs
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 clove garlic
- mixed dried herbs
- 200g wholewheat pasta (dry weight)
---

## Method

1. Mix the turkey mince, egg, breadcrumbs and herbs; shape into meatballs.
2. Bake or fry until browned and cooked through (about 12 minutes).
3. Simmer in the tomato sauce with garlic for 10 minutes.
4. Serve over cooked pasta.
