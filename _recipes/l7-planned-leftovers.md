## Method

1. When cooking T3, T7 or T12, portion out the extra for lunch straight away and refrigerate.
2. Reheat thoroughly until piping hot before eating.

## Notes

- The ragu (T12) is about 350 kcal with a big salad instead of spaghetti, which balances the bigger risotto (T11) that evening.
- Chilli or bolognese with rice or pasta is about 400 kcal.
