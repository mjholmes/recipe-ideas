ingredients:
- 200g yellow split peas, rinsed
- 900ml water
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, thumb of ginger, chopped
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 2 handfuls spinach
- 'per portion: 1/2 Greek flatbread and 1 tbsp fat-free yogurt'
---

## Method

1. Simmer the split peas in the water with the turmeric for 45-50 minutes until soft, adding water if needed.
2. Meanwhile fry the onion, garlic, ginger and spices in the oil for 5 minutes, then add the tomatoes for 5 minutes more.
3. Stir into the peas with the spinach until wilted, and season.
4. Serve with half a warmed flatbread and a spoon of yogurt.

## Notes

- freezes well.
