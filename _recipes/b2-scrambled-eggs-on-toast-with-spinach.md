## Method

1. Whisk the eggs with a splash of milk and seasoning.
2. Wilt the spinach in a non-stick pan for 1 minute, push to the side.
3. Add the eggs and scramble gently for 2-3 minutes.
4. Serve on toasted wholemeal bread.
