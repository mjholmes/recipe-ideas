- 1 large chicken breast (~200g), diced
- 3 rashers back bacon, fat trimmed, chopped
- 150g arborio rice
- 1 onion, chopped, and 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tsp oil
- 700ml hot chicken stock, plus a splash extra
- 150g frozen peas
- 20g parmesan, grated
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / Gas 6.
2. In a lidded ovenproof pan, fry the bacon and onion in the oil for 4 minutes. Add the chicken and garlic for 3 minutes, then stir in the rice for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the stock, cover and bake for 20 minutes.
4. Stir in the peas with a splash of stock and bake for 5 minutes more.
5. Stir in the parmesan and season.

## Notes

- baking it means no standing and stirring.
