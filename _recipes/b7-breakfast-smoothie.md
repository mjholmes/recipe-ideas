ingredients:
- 2 bananas
- 2 large handfuls spinach
- 300ml semi-skimmed milk
- 200g fat-free Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp oats
---

## Method

1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
2. Blend until smooth and pour into glasses.
