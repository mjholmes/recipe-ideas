## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan (or per the pastry pack).
2. Warm the filling in a pan until piping hot, adding a splash of stock if it's thick. Tip into a small ovenproof dish.
3. Cut the pastry into strips and lay as a lattice over the top, or cut a lid just big enough to sit on the filling with no overhang. Brush with egg or milk.
4. Bake for 20-25 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve with peas or greens.

## Notes

- the filling is homemade so calories can't be pinned down exactly, aim for a portion that keeps the plate around 550 kcal; it already contains potato, so no extra carbs are needed. Puff pastry is calorie-dense (~400 kcal/100g), so a third of a sheet split between two is about 200 kcal each; filo (3 sheets, ~100-120 kcal each) is the lighter option if you'd rather.
