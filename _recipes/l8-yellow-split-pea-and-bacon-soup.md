ingredients:
- 200g yellow split peas, rinsed
- 4 rashers back bacon, fat trimmed, diced
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, chopped
- 1 tsp oil
- 1.2 litres reduced-salt stock
- 1 bay leaf
---

## Method

1. Fry the bacon and veg in the oil for 5 minutes.
2. Add the split peas, stock and bay leaf. Simmer for 50-60 minutes, stirring now and then and skimming any foam, until the peas collapse.
3. Remove the bay leaf. Blitz half for a thicker texture if you like, and season.

## Notes

- freezes well. A slice of garlic bread alongside adds roughly 100 kcal (check the pack). Long simmer but little attention needed, so a good weekend job.
