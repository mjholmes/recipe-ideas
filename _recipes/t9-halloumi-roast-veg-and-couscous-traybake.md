- 125g lighter halloumi, sliced
- 1 red pepper, 1 courgette, 1 red onion, chopped
- 200g cherry tomatoes
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika or dried oregano
- 100g couscous and hot stock (per pack)
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 2 tbsp mixed seeds
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C / 200°C fan / Gas 7.
2. Toss the veg with the oil and paprika and roast for 15 minutes.
3. Add the halloumi and roast for another 10-12 minutes until golden.
4. Meanwhile pour the boiling stock over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes, then fluff up. Toast the seeds in a dry pan.
5. Toss everything together with the lemon juice.
