ingredients:
- 80g rolled oats
- 200g fat-free Greek yogurt
- 1 apple, grated (skin on)
- 100ml semi-skimmed milk
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tsp honey
- squeeze of lemon juice
---

## Method

1. Grate the apple and toss it with a squeeze of lemon juice to stop it browning.
2. Mix the oats, milk, yogurt, cinnamon and grated apple in two jars or bowls.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).
4. Stir and drizzle with honey before serving.
