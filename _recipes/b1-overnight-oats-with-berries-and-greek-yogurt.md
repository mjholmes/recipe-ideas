ingredients:
- 80g rolled oats
- 300g fat-free Greek yogurt
- 160g mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- 2 tsp honey
- 100ml semi-skimmed milk
---

## Method

1. Split the oats and milk between two jars or bowls, stir in half the yogurt.
2. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).
3. In the morning, top with the remaining yogurt, berries and a drizzle of honey.
