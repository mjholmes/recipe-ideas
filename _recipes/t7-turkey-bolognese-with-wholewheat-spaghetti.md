ingredients:
- 500g lean turkey mince
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 carrots, grated
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- mixed dried herbs
- 240g wholewheat spaghetti (dry weight)
---

## Method

1. Brown the mince in a large pan.
2. Add the onion, garlic and grated carrot, cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add the tomatoes, purée and herbs, simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Serve over spaghetti, portion the extra straight into the fridge for tomorrow's lunch.
