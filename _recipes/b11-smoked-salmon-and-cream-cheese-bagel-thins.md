- 60g light cream cheese
- 100g smoked salmon
- squeeze of lemon juice
- black pepper
- handful rocket or watercress (optional)
---

## Method

1. Toast the bagel thins.
2. Spread with the light cream cheese and top with the smoked salmon.
3. Finish with a squeeze of lemon, black pepper and a few rocket leaves if using.
