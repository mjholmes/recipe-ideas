- 4 eggs

- salt & pepper
- spray oil
- 200g tinned black or kidney beans, warmed
- 2 wholemeal wraps
- 2 tbsp salsa
- 30g reduced-fat cheddar, grated (optional)
---

## Method

1. Whisk the eggs with a splash of milk and seasoning; scramble in a non-stick pan with a little oil.
2. Warm the beans in a small pan or the microwave.
3. Warm the wraps, then fill each with scrambled egg, beans, salsa and a little grated cheese.
4. Roll up and serve.
