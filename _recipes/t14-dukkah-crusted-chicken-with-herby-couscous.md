- 20g hazelnuts or almonds
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp coriander seeds, pinch of salt
- 1 tbsp yogurt and a squeeze of lemon, for brushing
- 100g couscous and hot stock (per pack)
- cucumber, cherry tomatoes and parsley or mint, chopped
- 100g fat-free Greek yogurt with lemon, to serve
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / Gas 6.
2. Toast the nuts and seeds in a dry pan for 3-4 minutes, then crush in a bag with a rolling pin.
3. Brush the chicken with the yogurt and lemon, press the dukkah on top and bake for 20-22 minutes until cooked through.
4. Make the couscous with the stock and stir in the chopped veg and herbs.
5. Serve with a dollop of lemony yogurt.
